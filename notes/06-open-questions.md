## Foundations

### Where is the energy of the gravitational field?

There is no tensor $t_{\mu\nu}$ for gravitational energy density: the
equivalence principle lets you remove the gravitational field at any point.
Yet binary pulsars lose energy to gravitational waves. Global definitions
(ADM mass, Bondi mass) work at infinity; pseudotensors (Landau–Lifshitz,
Einstein) depend on coordinates; quasi-local masses (Brown–York, Hawking) are a
compromise. Which is "the" answer, if any?

### What does general covariance actually require?

Kretschmann (1917) objected that *any* theory can be written covariantly, so
covariance alone has no physical content. Einstein's hole argument
(1913–1915) suggests the real content is that spacetime points have no identity
apart from the metric. What is left of the principle?

### Is $\Lambda$ geometry or energy?

Moved to the right-hand side, $\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}$ looks like vacuum energy with
$p = -\rho c^2$. Quantum field theory estimates of that energy overshoot the
observed value by a famously enormous factor (often quoted as up to $10^{120}$).
Is that a problem for GR, for QFT, or for the way we combine them?

## Mathematics

### Is the initial-value problem well posed?

Choquet-Bruhat (1952) proved local existence and uniqueness in harmonic gauge.
What do the constraints mean physically, and why did stable numerical
evolutions of binary black holes take until 2005 (Pretorius; then the
moving-puncture method of Campanelli et al. and Baker et al.)?

### Singularities and cosmic censorship

The Penrose (1965) and Hawking–Penrose (1970) theorems say singularities are
generic once trapped surfaces form. Weak cosmic censorship says they are always
hidden behind horizons. Known counterexamples need fine-tuning. Is
censorship true?

### Uniqueness, and how to escape it

Lovelock's theorem ([01](01-the-field-equations.md)) pins down the field
equations in four dimensions. Each modified-gravity program (scalar-tensor,
$f(R)$, massive gravity, Gauss–Bonnet in higher dimensions) breaks one of its
assumptions. Which loophole is most physically motivated?

## Physics and observation

### Dark matter and dark energy: new stuff or new gravity?

Flat rotation curves and accelerated expansion can be fit with unseen matter
and $\Lambda$, or by changing the left-hand side. What observations
discriminate (cluster lensing, the CMB, GW170817's speed limit on many
modified theories)?

### Where does GR stop?

GR is non-renormalizable as a quantum field theory (pure gravity diverges at
two loops: Goroff–Sagnotti 1986), but works well as an effective field theory
below the Planck scale. Semiclassical gravity sets $G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G \langle T_{\mu\nu} \rangle$.
When does that break, and what replaces it?

### Testing strong-field gravity

Black-hole ringdowns, Event Horizon Telescope images, and pulsar timing all
probe regimes where $\Phi/c^2 \sim 1$. Has anything deviated from Kerr yet,
and what would a deviation look like?
